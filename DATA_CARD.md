## Known limitations

- The data is synthetic. Typology mix, rates and timing reflect the generator, not a real bank.
- Most volume falls in the first days; the remainder is a sparse tail dominated by laundering.
  Splits are therefore cut by transaction count, not by time span.
- Not all laundering belongs to a typology block; unattributed laundering is reported as
  `UNATTRIBUTED` and counted in every headline metric.
- Amounts are in many currencies and are never summed across currencies.
